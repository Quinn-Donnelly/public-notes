Z-Score

Sep 15, 20252 min read

The Z score is the number of standard deviations a given observation is above or below the mean.1

z=σx−xˉ​​

The z represents z score, xˉ2 represents the mean, and σ3 for standard deviation.

You can solve for any of the above so long as you have the other two. AND, if you have two observations and it’s z score you can calculate the mean and standard deviation by using systems of equations.45

z1​z2​x1​−μx2​−μ(x1​−x2​)σμ​=σx1​−μ​=σx2​−μ​=z1​σ=z2​σ=(z1​−z2​)σ=z1​−z2​x1​−x2​​=x1​−z1​σ​

Z scores provide a means to compare results from different measurement sources. EX. If someone takes the ACT and another person takes the SAT you can’t directly compare but if you calculate z-score you can.6
