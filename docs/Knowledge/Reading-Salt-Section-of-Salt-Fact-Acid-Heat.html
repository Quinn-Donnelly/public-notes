2025-09-27 My recollection of the reading

The core of the section on salt is to

  1. Stress the importance on seasoning food
    1. Seasoning referring to salting food
  2. Show the various methods and implications of various salt delivery mechanisms in food
  3. Give some guiding tips for the important components of salting

Keep two salts on hand Density of salt effects dissolution time

Ways to apply salt

  1. Direct
  2. Salt water soak
    1. Pasta water / brine
  3. other ingredients bringing the salt

Equally important is time. Salting before vs salting later. Adding salt at the table is nearly always a downside. Salting before is good as it allows diffusion to happen and salt the food more thoroughly instead of being built up on surface.

Too early though and you have side effects that could be disadvantageous such as creating rubbery textures on fish / drying them out.

Making chicken stock then slowly adding more salt was a recommended practice as was creating Caesar dressing.

Comparing my notes

Use salt to reduce bitterness Salt helps meat retain moisture

Important to remember with salt and fats that the salt will need a water to help it dissolve. So lean meats will have greater ability to carry salt due to higher water content. 1

Salt and eggs - salt will decrease the cook time on eggs, the proteins come together at a lower temperature with salt. 2

Conflicting information on the effects of salt on pectin Salt weakens pectin which will help get it off vegetables - it’s an indigestible carbohydrate that you don’t really want. 3

Mushrooms should be salted late in the game else they will release too much water as they are made up of 80% water.45

Salt strengthens gluten, add salt to bread early as well as it takes time to dissolve due to low overall water content.6

Osmosis / diffusion play a key role in veg being cooked in water. If there are more minerals in the veg when boiled then the minerals will be pulled out. If more in water they will be pulled in. 7

0.5 - 1% salinity recommendation for boiling in water.

Salt diffusion is best encouraged89

  1. Time
  2. Temperature
  3. Water

1% salt by weight for meats, veg, and grains10 2% salinity for blanching and pasta. 1112

(helpful guide on pg. 43 for salt measurements w/ different kinds of salt)

A one off recommendation on Terricherry Peppercorn as the superior peppercorn due to ripening on the vine longer and therefore develop more of a flavor. 13

There was also another mention of spice grinder being preferred due to spices tasting better when ground just before use.
